# Methodology

MaskLab is a controlled behavioral evaluation tool. A role or framing intervention can support a scoped comparison of observable responses under the recorded protocol; it does not identify neurons, training causes, faithful internal reasoning or universal model traits.

Read the canonical experiment protocol for paired controls, exact metric denominators, question-cluster intervals, repetitions, holdout discipline and uncertainty. Keep exploratory and confirmatory records distinct. Interpret null/negative findings and provider errors openly. Outputs are only as valid as their question set, answer keys, extraction protocol and serving context.
