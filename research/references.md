Research starting points (not a claim of exhaustive literature review):
- When “A Helpful Assistant” Is Not Really Helpful: Personas in System Prompts Do Not Improve Performances of Large Language Models — https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.10054 . Motivation to test role effects rather than assume benefits.
- Towards Understanding Sycophancy in Language Models — https://www.anthropic.com/research/towards-understanding-sycophancy-in-language-models . Motivation to separate truth/answer correctness from stated-belief agreement.
- Principled Personas: Defining and Measuring the Intended Effects of Persona Prompting on Task Performance — https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.1364.pdf . Relevant framing for expertise, robustness and fidelity.
