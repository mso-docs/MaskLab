# Leakage and analysis integrity

Freeze development/held-out question IDs before evaluation. Never send answer keys to an absent-belief or incorrect-belief condition through accidental prompt/context inclusion. The correct-belief condition intentionally contains its designated answer and must be labeled accordingly. Each trial is a fresh conversation. Keep underlying task, choices and question ordering consistent within declared comparisons.

Do not tune prompts after inspecting held-out results without marking a new exploratory protocol and acknowledging reuse. Role/frame variants are not independent samples of the question population. Imported datasets require provenance and answer verification. Preserve original records, failed/negative findings, predeclared exclusions and the analysis version; no post-hoc cherry-picking or changing a key to match model output.
